About The Foundation

Humanity Should Never Be Forgotten.

The Komey Foundation is a humanitarian and community development initiative driven by compassion, service and a commitment to making a meaningful difference.

Our focus is on people experiencing poverty, homelessness, hunger and social vulnerability. We believe that support should go beyond words. It should be demonstrated through meaningful action and consistent community involvement.

Starting in South Africa, our ambition is to establish a growing network of community support initiatives across Africa, reaching people and communities who need support most.

Our African Vision

Starting in South Africa. Growing Across Africa.

Poverty and hunger do not recognise borders. Our long-term ambition is to build a foundation that can support vulnerable communities throughout Africa.

We want to develop local partnerships, mobilise volunteers, attract corporate sponsorship and establish sustainable humanitarian programmes in the communities we serve.

  • Community feeding initiatives
  • Support for homeless individuals
  • Food assistance for vulnerable families
  • Education and youth empowerment
  • Cross-border humanitarian partnerships
