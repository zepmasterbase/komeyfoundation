Volunteer opportunities

Find your way to make an impact.

Our volunteer opportunities are designed around practical community needs and the different skills people can bring.

01

Community Feeding

Assist with food preparation, meal distribution, feeding events and community outreach.

02

Homeless Outreach

Help organise outreach activities and distribute essential supplies to people experiencing homelessness.

03

Family Support

Help coordinate initiatives that support vulnerable families and less privileged communities.

04

Youth & Skills Development

Share your knowledge, professional experience and skills to help young people access opportunities.

05

Fundraising & Partnerships

Help connect the foundation with donors, businesses, sponsors and organisations that can support our work.

06

Digital & Administration

Support communications, design, social media, research, administration and digital projects.
